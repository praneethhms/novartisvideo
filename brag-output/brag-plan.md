# /brag plan: Driver Safety Programme pilot

**Source:** Novartis_Pilot_Overview_Compact.pptx + Novartis_Pilot_Review_Novartis_Format_v2.pptx

**What it is:** A 66-day telematics pilot in which the SafetyConnect smartphone app scored the driving of 20 Novartis India field associates and showed where road risk sits.

**Who it's for:** HSE and division leaders deciding whether to take the pilot to the full field force.

**Angle:** The risk was invisible until a phone in a pocket counted it. 7,716 at-risk moments, flagged before any incident, and most of it comes down to two habits.

**Hook:** Black screen. "7,716" counts up in orange. "at-risk driving moments. Flagged before any incident."

**Highlights:**
1. 66 days · 20 field associates · 11,843 km · 1,359 trips, all captured automatically. No hardware.
2. Where the risk sits: Harsh Braking 53.7% + Phone Usage 22.5% → 76% of all events in two behaviours.
3. 19 of 20 drivers score Good. Average 86.3.

**Punchline:** Detect → Aware → Correct → Reinforce. "From pilot to programme."

**Tone:** `polished`: restrained, deck-faithful, long holds, soft fades.

**Identity:** White #FCFCFC, ink #161616, orange #FF4E00, panels #F2F2F2, greys #484848/#7A7A7A, Arial (rendered with the metric-identical Liberation Sans), the deck's Novartis wordmark and blue-to-orange gradient bar.

**Format:** 1920×1080, 30fps, about 21s.
